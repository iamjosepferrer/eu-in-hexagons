How this was made

Method: residents in Eurostat's 1 km census grid, added up in H3 hexagons. A 1 km cell belongs to the smallest hexagon its centre falls in. Hexagons are drawn whole, so they reach over the sea and across borders.

Source: Eurostat, Census 2021 population grid and NUTS 2024, © European Union. H3 by Uber. Borders, roads and cities: Natural Earth.

Comments: only residents of EU countries are counted. The Canaries, Madeira, the Azores and France's five overseas regions are left out. Hexagons of one size differ in area by up to a third, and where two sizes meet they leave small gaps, because seven small hexagons don't trace the big one exactly.
